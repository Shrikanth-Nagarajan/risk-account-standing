# Account standing

A concept prototype for making risk actions (reserves, payout holds, reviews) understandable and fixable for the businesses they affect, with a Risk Ops view of the same case.

- **Prototype:** https://shrikanth-nagarajan.github.io/risk-account-standing/
- **One-page brief:** https://shrikanth-nagarajan.github.io/risk-account-standing/brief.html

Use the bar at the top to switch between the business and Risk Ops views, the UK and Ireland, and three scenarios: a rolling reserve after a pre-order spike, payouts paused for a restricted review, and nothing to action. Changes carry across views, so evidence sent as the business shows up in the analyst's case, and the analyst's decision shows up for the business.

Built by Shri Nagarajan with Claude Code. Not affiliated with Stripe. Every business, person and number is made up. Regulatory notes (UK GDPR as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, the GDPR, tipping-off rules, ombudsman eligibility) are my reading and would need checking with Legal.

No build step and no dependencies. Two static HTML files.
